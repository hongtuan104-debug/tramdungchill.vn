Christmas in Da Lat 2026: Snow Photo Spot and a Christmas Train

The Christmas in Da Lat 2026 setup at Trạm Dừng Chill is now lit up: a Christmas train, snow-covered pine trees and a falling-snow photo corner right beside the greenhouse valley.

TL;DR: For Christmas 2026, Trạm Dừng Chill has a snow-covered Christmas corner with a lit-up locomotive, a big pine tree and a falling-snow effect for photos. Come from around 16:00 to have BBQ, watch the sunset, then take your photos once all the Christmas lights are on.

Christmas in Da Lat already comes with just the right chill to make you wrap up in a scarf, sit close to the grill and stay a little longer with someone you love. This year, Trạm Dừng Chill has built a Christmas train right in the middle of its space overlooking the greenhouse valley. From your table you can move through three scenes in one evening: the sunset, the snow-covered Christmas train, and the sea of greenhouse lights after dark.

What's on at Trạm Dừng Chill for Christmas in Da Lat 2026?

This year's highlight is a snow-covered Christmas train bearing the Trạm Dừng Chill name. The locomotive, the tracks and the carriages are all outlined in warm yellow lights. Next to it stand a big pine tree, clusters of snow-covered trees and a photo path with a falling-snow effect. The space is styled in white, green and gold, so night photos still show people, scenery and the restaurant's name clearly.

This isn't a small backdrop set up indoors. The decorated area is outdoors and links the tracks and pine trees with the valley view behind, so your photos have depth and still feel unmistakably Da Lat.

The best photo angles in the Christmas snow area

If you're short on time, shoot in the order below to get a full set of wide, medium and portrait shots:

  1. Wide shot from above: fit in the whole locomotive, the carriages, the tracks and the pine tree.
  2. Next to the pine tree: place your subject in the gap between the locomotive and the tree so both the person and the lights stand out.
  3. Under an umbrella while the snow falls: works well with a light-colored dress, a red scarf or a neutral-toned coat.
  4. Close-ups with the snow: use burst mode to catch the moment snowflakes drift past the lens.

What time should you come for both a good meal and great photos?

The easiest window is from 16:00 until after 18:30: you get enough light for sunset photos, time for your meal, and a glowing backdrop for your evening Christmas shots.

We're open 15:00–23:00 every day. The vintage train passes the restaurant between 16:30 and 21:25; times may change with the operating schedule. If you'd like to catch the real train as well as the Christmas train display, see our guide to train-spotting in Da Lat at Trạm Dừng Chill.

A simple Christmas plan for one evening

The best plan is to arrive around 16:00, choose a table facing the valley and start grilling while the light is still golden. After sunset, walk over to the Christmas train area for photos just as the lights come on. Head back to your table when the greenhouses below glow into a “sea of stars”, order something hot and stay until the city lights up.

Groups can check out the menu of more than 70 dishes in advance. Couples planning a special evening can look at our dates and proposals in Da Lat page. If you're coming with family, the article on BBQ restaurants in Da Lat for families has more tips on choosing the right seats.

Booking for the Christmas season

The best view spots fill up fast on weekends and in the days around 24/12. On the night of 24/12 there's no surcharge. Most guests spend 150,000–400,000 VND per person, VAT included. We're about 7 km from central Da Lat, with free parking for motorbikes and cars; at weekend peak times you may need to park around 100–200 m further away.

We recommend you book directly on our website with a note like “Christmas visit, table near the photo area”, or call +84 989 765 070. Trạm Dừng Chill is at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward.

A warm scarf, someone by your side and an unhurried evening — that's all it takes for the Christmas train at Trạm Dừng Chill to become a lovely memory of Christmas in Da Lat 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Where can you take Christmas 2026 photos with snow and pine trees in Da Lat?

Trạm Dừng Chill at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward has a Christmas train area, snow-covered pine trees and an outdoor falling-snow effect for photos.

Is there a fee to take photos in the Christmas area at Trạm Dừng Chill?

No. Anyone can come and take photos at the Christmas train area — you don't have to eat or drink here. It gets quite busy on weekends and in the days around 24/12; if you'd like to stay for dinner, book a table ahead.

What time should you arrive at Trạm Dừng Chill for great Christmas photos?

Arrive around 16:00 for sunset photos, have your meal, then shoot the Christmas area from around 18:30, when the decorations and the greenhouses below are both lit up.

Is there a surcharge at Trạm Dừng Chill on the night of 24/12?

No. Trạm Dừng Chill has no surcharge on the night of 24/12 and no surcharge for view tables. Book early, as this is a peak date.

What are Trạm Dừng Chill's opening hours during the Christmas season?

We're open from 15:00 to 23:00 every day at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward.

Can you see the real train and take photos with the Christmas train on the same visit?

Yes. The vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat train runs right below the restaurant between 16:30 and 21:25, while the Christmas train display is part of the restaurant's decorated area. Actual train times may change with the operating schedule.
