Free Birthday Setup in Da Lat — Which Restaurants Decorate Your Party Table for Free?

Looking for a restaurant in Da Lat that sets up birthdays for free? Trạm Dừng Chill decorates your party table for free (decorative flowers, a greeting board) — with views of the train, the sunset and the greenhouse sea of stars.

In Da Lat, Tiệm Nướng Trạm Dừng Chill (111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward) offers free birthday and anniversary setups: your party table is dressed with decorative flowers and a greeting board at no decoration charge — we only take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date, refunded after your meal. You pay for what you order, usually 95,000–300,000 VND per person including VAT, and you get to enjoy BBQ on a balcony overlooking the vintage train, with the greenhouse sea of stars as a natural backdrop. The restaurant is rated 4.8/5 from 7,344 reviews on Google Maps (as of 18/10/2026). There are two BBQ restaurants side by side here: Trạm Dừng Chill at No. 111 and Tiệm Nướng & Chill Xóm Lèo (our sister restaurant) at No. 113 — both look out over the greenhouse valley.

What does the free birthday setup at Trạm Dừng Chill include?

The biggest difference: the decoration is free of charge. Because we need to prepare in advance, we take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date and refund it after your meal — regular table bookings need no deposit. When you book, just mention "birthday setup" or "anniversary setup" and our team will have your party table ready before you arrive. The free decoration includes:

  • A greeting board (Happy Birthday / Happy Anniversary) placed right on your party table.
  • Decorative flowers on the party table.

No decoration fee and no minimum spend; we serve à la carte only, with no combos or fixed set menus. The free setup is our standing policy, not a seasonal promotion.

A natural backdrop for your party: the train, the sunset, the greenhouses

The restaurant sits right on the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat railway (a roughly 7 km stretch still running tourist trains, part of a line built by the French), and the train runs right below it. A party here has as many as 3 moments to serve as a backdrop:

  • Train view: grill while you watch the vintage train roll by and hear its whistle.
  • Valley sunset: golden afternoon light from 15:00, with the sunset settling in from around 16:30; the 180° view turns the whole valley gold.
  • The greenhouse "sea of stars": around 18:30, thousands of greenhouses in the valley light up all at once like a sea of stars — the most beautiful moment to blow out the candles on your cake.

Choosing the best time to blow out the candles

A small tip: arriving around 16:00 is the "golden window" — you'll be there when the trains start passing the restaurant (16:30), watch the sunset, then wait until 18:30 to blow out the candles over the greenhouse sea of stars.

The vintage train passes the restaurant between 16:30 and 21:25. Note: we're often full between 17:30 and 19:00, so book your party earlier or choose 19:30 onwards for a more relaxed time.

Frequently asked questions

Which restaurant in Da Lat offers a free birthday setup?

Is there a charge for a birthday setup at Trạm Dừng Chill?

No. The decoration is completely free, with no minimum spend. You only pay for what you order, usually 95,000–300,000 VND per person. Because we need to prepare the decoration in advance, we take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date and refund it after your meal — regular table bookings need no deposit. See the menu.

How many people can a birthday party be for?

Trạm Dừng Chill has nearly 250 tables and about 1,500 seats, and takes everything from tables for 2 to large groups; it's all à la carte, so you can order to suit your budget. Groups of more than 10 pay a deposit, and our staff will tell you the amount when they confirm; large groups should let us know in advance so we can arrange an area and good view spots.

How far ahead should I book to get a setup?

Let us know 1–2 days ahead, and earlier for weekends. When you book, be sure to say 'birthday setup' or 'anniversary setup' so we can have the decoration ready before you arrive.

How do I book a party at Trạm Dừng Chill?

Fill in the booking form on our website or message us on WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 with the number of guests, the date and time, and the occasion. Our staff will confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within about 15 minutes (during opening hours).

What's the best time to blow out the candles and enjoy the view?

Be there around 16:00 to catch the trains from when they start passing the restaurant (16:30) and watch the sunset; around 18:30 the greenhouses in the valley light up into a 'sea of stars' — the most magical moment to blow out the candles. Note that 17:30–19:00 is usually fully booked.

Is there car parking?

Trạm Dừng Chill has free parking for both motorbikes and cars right at the restaurant. We're at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. At weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away. We open at 15:00, so arriving early makes parking easier; Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.
