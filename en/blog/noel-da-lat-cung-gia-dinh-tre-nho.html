Christmas in Da Lat for Families With Young Children: When to Arrive, Where to Sit and What Kids Can Eat

Tips for Christmas in Da Lat with young children: come in the afternoon so the kids can see the Christmas train area while it's still warm, sit in a covered row, order non-spicy dishes for the little ones and head back before the night turns cold.

TL;DR: For Christmas in Da Lat with young children, come to Trạm Dừng Chill in the 15:00–17:00 window: it's still quiet and warm, and kids can see the Christmas train area for free and watch the real vintage train pass by from around 16:30. Sit in a covered row, order a few non-spicy dishes for the kids, ask for blankets and a charcoal heater when it gets cold, and avoid 17:30–19:00, when we're usually full.

For many families, Christmas is when kids get to see a Christmas tree, reindeer and “snow” for the first time. Da Lat has all that atmosphere, but also two things that wear parents out: it gets dark and cold quickly, and the city center is crowded. This article gathers what our team usually tells families with young children during the Christmas season.

Christmas in Da Lat with kids: what time should you go?

Families with young children should go for Christmas in the afternoon, in the 15:00–17:00 window, when it's still light and warm, the decorated area is less crowded and everyone can get back before the cold night sets in.

  • 15:00–16:00: we've just opened, it's still quiet and the staff have more time on their hands; kids can take photos at the Christmas train area while the colors are still vivid.
  • From around 16:30: sunset begins and the real vintage train passes the restaurant during the 16:30–21:25 window, usually when kids get most excited.
  • Around 18:30: the greenhouses in the valley light up; if you want the kids to see it, arrive from 16:00 and stay until then, rather than arriving right at peak time.

Between 17:30 and 19:00 we're usually full, and it's fairly noisy and cramped for managing young children. The night of 24/12 is even busier, so if you don't have to go on that exact date, choose another afternoon: the Christmas area is open through 31/12/2026. And if the whole family wants to make a full evening of 24/12, see Christmas in Da Lat: where to go and what to eat.

What does the Christmas train area have for children?

The Christmas train area at Trạm Dừng Chill has a display locomotive and carriages outlined in lights, a track, rows of snow-covered pines, decorative reindeer and a corner with a falling-snow effect; anyone can come in to take photos, free of charge.

For young children, the area is like an outdoor Christmas card: a train, snow and reindeer to point at and pose with. Keep kids on the photo path and don't let them climb on the displays, the pine trees or the decorative fences. The Christmas train display is only a decoration; the real vintage train runs on the track below the restaurant, and you can see it from the railing rows.

Which row to sit in and what to wear with kids?

Families with young children should sit in the covered railing row or the neighboring row B: they already have a roof and are less windy than the open-air railing seats, while still giving a wide view of the sunset and the greenhouses.

Da Lat evenings are cold, especially at the outdoor seats. Besides a warm jacket, a woolly hat and socks for your child, you can ask the staff for blankets and a charcoal heater brought right to your table. We have free WiFi throughout the restaurant, including the outdoor railing rows.

What to order for the kids and the whole family?

For young children, order a few easy, non-spicy dishes first, like fries, fried rice or stir-fried noodles, then order grilled dishes and hotpot for the adults.

  • For the kids: French Fries, Sweet Potato Balls, Highland Fried Rice, Stir-Fried Beef Noodles.
  • Easy-to-eat grilled dishes: Kobe Beef with Salted Egg Cheese (rich, not spicy), Enoki Beef Rolls (small pieces, easy to share).
  • For the whole family: a Chicken Hotpot with É Leaves or a Sturgeon Hotpot, for something hot and brothy.
  • Best avoided for kids: the chili-salt grilled dishes, such as Grilled Chicken Feet with Chili Salt and Grilled Chicken Wings with Chili Salt.

We don't have a special Christmas menu and don't sell combos to individual diners. Typical spending is 150,000–400,000 VND per person, VAT included; for a family of 4 that comes to about 600,000–1,600,000 VND. No surcharge on 24/12. Open the menu at home beforehand to pick dishes for the kids.

What parents should know before going

Trạm Dừng Chill welcomes families with young children, but there are a few things parents should know beforehand: the charcoal grills are always hot, the railing rows look straight down onto the track, and we don't have a dedicated play area for kids.

  • Charcoal: children under 3 should sit on the inner side, away from the grill.
  • Railway track: don't let kids climb the railing or run toward the track, especially when a train is about to pass.
  • Our staff help keep an eye on little ones while parents eat, but that's help from our serving staff, not a childcare service; parents remain in charge of their children.
  • The way back: the road through the Trai Mat area is quite quiet and dark at night; if you're on a motorbike with kids, head back before 21:00.

More details on seating, kid-friendly dishes and birthdays for little ones are in our article on BBQ restaurants in Da Lat for families.

Booking a table for families at Christmas

Families coming during the Christmas season should book ahead and add the note “with young children, prefer a covered row” so we can arrange a sheltered spot that works for the kids.

Book on our website or call +84 989 765 070; we confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within about 15 minutes if you book during opening hours (15:00–23:00). Regular tables don't need a deposit. If your trip falls on your child's birthday, we decorate the table for free (a greeting board and decorative flowers), with a refundable 200,000 VND deposit, returned after your meal. Trạm Dừng Chill is at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, about 7 km from the center, with free parking for motorbikes and cars.

An afternoon with a train, snow and reindeer, plus a warm dinner, is all it takes for your child's first Christmas in Da Lat to come with lots of lovely photos.

Frequently asked questions

What time should you arrive at Trạm Dừng Chill for Christmas in Da Lat with young children?

The 15:00–17:00 window works best: it's still quiet, light and warm, and kids can see the Christmas train area and watch the vintage train pass by from around 16:30. Between 17:30 and 19:00 we're usually full.

Is there a fee for children to enter the Christmas train area?

No. The Christmas train area is open to everyone for free photos through 31/12/2026 — you don't have to eat or drink here.

Do you have dishes for kids who can't eat spicy food?

You can order French Fries, Sweet Potato Balls, Highland Fried Rice, Stir-Fried Beef Noodles or Kobe Beef with Salted Egg Cheese; it's best to avoid the chili-salt grilled dishes.

Is it cold for kids sitting outdoors in the evening?

Yes, Da Lat evenings are quite cold. Bring warm clothes for your child and sit in a covered row; we have blankets and charcoal heaters we can bring to your table — just tell the staff.

Do you have a play area for children?

No. We don't have a dedicated play area; our staff help keep an eye on little ones while parents eat, but parents remain in charge of their children.
