Team Building in Da Lat: A Scenic BBQ Restaurant for Large Groups — Book Ahead

Looking for a team-building venue in Da Lat for a large group? Tiệm Nướng Trạm Dừng Chill can put many tables together, with a train view + sunset + sea of stars, at 150,000–400,000 VND per person.

For large groups who want to eat and bond at the same time, Tiệm Nướng Trạm Dừng Chill (111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward) is a team-building stop to round off the day: we can put many tables together so the whole group sits as one, with grill-at-your-table BBQ at 150,000–400,000 VND per person and a view of the vintage train running right below the restaurant. We're rated 4.8/5 from 7,344 reviews on Google Maps (as of 18/10/2026). If your group wants to compare a few places, Tiệm Nướng & Chill Xóm Lèo (our sister restaurant), right next door at 113 Huynh Tan Phat, also takes in the valley view.

Why is Da Lat great for team building?

Da Lat is cool all year round, with plenty of outdoor spaces and beautiful scenery — ideal for team-bonding activities. From Ho Chi Minh City it's about a 50-minute flight to Lien Khuong Airport (not counting the time to get to the airport and into the city), or about 6 hours by road, depending on the route and departure time. A full team-building itinerary usually combines active daytime activities (trekking, mini games, workshops) with an evening gathered around a BBQ feast. Dinner is when the group truly “comes together” — and that's where Trạm Dừng Chill shines.

A BBQ feast — the easiest way to bring a team together

Nothing breaks the ice between colleagues faster than sitting around a grill together. At Trạm Dừng Chill, your group can book several tables side by side in the outdoor area, grilling your own food while watching the sunset over the valley. We have three signature views that few places offer all together:

  • Train view: the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat railway (a roughly 7 km stretch that still runs tourist trains, part of a line built by the French) runs right below the restaurant. The moment a train passes, whistle blowing, always gets the whole group excited.
  • Valley sunset: golden afternoon light from 15:00, with the sunset settling in from around 16:30; a 180° view down over the valley and its greenhouse farms.
  • The greenhouse “sea of stars”: around 18:30, thousands of greenhouses light up at once, twinkling like a sea of stars across the valley — a stunning backdrop for group photos.

For groups celebrating a special occasion (a project milestone, a team member's birthday, a company anniversary), we offer free party-table decoration (a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date, refunded after your meal) — a nice extra to make the evening more memorable. See more ideas on our team building and birthday party pages.

Time your visit so the whole group can see the train

If you want the whole group to “catch” the moment a train passes, time your arrival. The window when trains pass the restaurant:

The vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat train passes the restaurant within the 16:30 – 21:25 window.

The golden window for groups: arrive around 16:00 to catch the trains from when they start passing the restaurant (16:30), then stay on for the sunset. Note that between 17:30 and 19:00 we're usually full, so large groups should come earlier or from 19:30. Full timing details are on our page about watching the trains pass the restaurant.

How do you book for a large group?

Large groups should book ahead, especially on weekends, when we fill up easily. How to book:

  • Fill in the booking form on our website with the number of people and your preferred time; we'll confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within about 15 minutes (during opening hours).
  • Or call/WhatsApp +84 989 765 070 to tell us about your group and the occasion (if you'd like decorations set up).
  • We're open 15:00–23:00 daily; groups who want to see the whole sunset should arrive in the afternoon.

Frequently asked questions

Does Trạm Dừng Chill take large team-building groups?

Yes. Trạm Dừng Chill has nearly 250 tables and about 1,500 seats; large groups can book several tables together in the outdoor area. Groups of more than 10 pay a deposit, and our staff will tell you the amount when confirming your date. Book ahead through the form on our website or on WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 with the number of people and your preferred time; we confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within about 15 minutes (during opening hours).

How much does a group meal at Trạm Dừng Chill cost per person?

Overall spending ranges from 150,000–400,000 VND per person depending on what you order, VAT included. Groups should order plenty of dishes to share; see individual dish prices on our menu page.

What time should our group arrive to see the train pass?

Trains pass the restaurant area within the 16:30 – 21:25 window. Arrive around 16:00 to catch them from when they start passing the restaurant (16:30), then stay on for the sunset.

Can you help decorate if our group is celebrating a special occasion?

Yes. Trạm Dừng Chill offers free party-table decoration for occasions such as birthdays and anniversaries; we take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date and refund it after your meal. Message us ahead on WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 so we can prepare; see more on our birthday party page.

What are your opening hours, and where are you?

Trạm Dừng Chill is at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, open 15:00–23:00 daily. Groups who want to see the train around 16:30 and then watch the sunset should arrive from about 16:00.

Do groups need to book early on weekends?

Yes, book early. We fill up easily on weekends, especially 17:30–19:00, when we usually don't take new bookings. Large groups should come earlier or from 19:30, and reserve ahead through the booking form.

Is there car parking?

Trạm Dừng Chill has free parking for both motorbikes and cars right at the restaurant. We're at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. At weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away. We open at 15:00, so arriving early makes parking easier; Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.
