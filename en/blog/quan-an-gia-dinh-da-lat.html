Family-Friendly BBQ in Da Lat — Staff Help Keep an Eye on the Kids

Worried about juggling small kids at a BBQ dinner in Da Lat? At Trạm Dừng Chill our staff help keep an eye on little ones, and there's spacious outdoor seating, a covered area for rainy days and free car parking.

Going out for BBQ with small kids is always a juggling act: parents have to grill, serve the kids and keep an eye on them running around — by the end of the meal the food is cold and you've barely had a bite. Tiệm Nướng Trạm Dừng Chill (111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward) solves exactly that with something very simple: our staff help keep an eye on your little one while you eat. The restaurant is rated 4.8/5 from 7,344 reviews on Google Maps (as of 18/10/2026), is open 15:00–23:00 every day, and spending runs 95,000–300,000 VND per person, VAT included. Right next door at number 113 is Tiệm Nướng & Chill Xóm Lèo (our sister restaurant), another BBQ spot with a train view if the family would like another option.

Staff help keep an eye on your little one while you eat

Something our staff often tell guests is: "Come have BBQ, we will look after your kids." In practice, when a table has small children, our staff help keep an eye on them and play with them around the table so the parents can enjoy the whole meal while the food is still hot.

To be clear: this is help from our serving staff, not a professional childcare service, and we don't have a separate play area. Parents are still responsible for watching their children — especially since the grills use charcoal, so there's always fire and hot surfaces. But simply having one more adult keeping an eye out is enough to completely change the experience of a family meal.

Spacious outdoor seating — still fine when it rains

We have outdoor tables with a 180° view of the valley, airy enough that kids don't feel cooped up like in a closed room. When it rains — which happens a lot in Da Lat — we have a large covered area that still looks out over the valley and the greenhouses lighting up, so a sudden shower won't ruin the family's plans.

Da Lat sits at an altitude of about 1,500m above sea level with a mild mountain climate all year round, so grilling outdoors never feels hot and stuffy — important when you're with small children. On the flip side, evenings get quite cold: we bring blankets and charcoal heaters right to your table, just ask our staff. When it rains, every guest gets a free raincoat to take home.

Which row is easiest with small children?

Tables at the restaurant are arranged in rows, each row being a line of tables all facing the same direction. One thing parents should know before messaging us to book: all tables in the same row share the same view. No need to fight for the first table in a row — whichever table you get in that row looks out on the same scene.

For families with small children, we usually suggest the covered railing row or row B right next to it: already roofed, less windy than sitting at the open-air railing, and still with a sweeping view of the sunset and the greenhouse valley. The open-air railing row beside the tracks is our most requested row — on weekends and in high season it is usually booked out 2–4 weeks ahead, so if the family really wants to sit right by the tracks, lock in your date early. Booking late but still want your little one to see the trains? Try row G right next to it, which shares the same train view.

Regular table bookings need no deposit — just fill in the booking form on our website or message us on WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 with the date and time, the number of people, how many children are coming and the row you'd like. Groups of more than 10 do pay a deposit; our staff will tell you the amount when you confirm your date.

What to order for small children?

Our menu has over 70 dishes, mostly grill-at-your-table BBQ and hotpot. For small children, easy-to-eat, non-spicy options include:

  • French Fries (70,000 VND) and Sweet Potato Balls (70,000 VND) — sides that almost every kid will happily eat.
  • Highland Fried Rice (140,000 VND) or Seafood Fried Rice (120,000 VND) — something filling for the kids while mom and dad are still grilling.
  • Stir-Fried Beef Noodles (135,000 VND) — easy to eat and not spicy.
  • Kobe Beef with Salted Egg Cheese (209,000 VND) — our signature dish; its rich, cheesy flavor suits kids better than the spicy marinated dishes.
  • Enoki Beef Rolls (137,000 VND) — small pieces that are easy to share.

If your child can't handle spice, skip the chili-salt dishes such as the grilled chicken feet and chicken wings with chili salt. For drinks there are hot teas, iced teas, sodas and non-alcoholic soft drinks for the whole family. Extra Vegetables are just 20,000 VND if you want to balance the meal.

All 70-plus dishes and their prices are on the menu on our website — it's worth looking through it at home and settling on one or two dishes for your child, so you can order in one go when you sit down instead of flipping through the menu while soothing a little one. Older kids waiting while their parents grill can use the free WiFi that covers the whole restaurant, including the open-air railing rows — every row gets a signal, so there's no need to move.

What time to arrive with small children

We open at 15:00. For families with small children, 15:00–17:00 is the best time to come:

  • It's still quiet, so our staff have free hands to help keep an eye on the kids and serve you more attentively.
  • It's still light, so it's easier to watch the kids as they play around the table.
  • You finish early, so the family heads home before the little ones get sleepy.
  • Golden afternoon light covers the valley, and the sunset starts from around 16:30.

Between 17:30 and 19:00 we're usually full and don't take new bookings, and it gets fairly noisy and crowded for managing small children. If you want the family to see the greenhouses light up (around 18:30), come from 16:00 and stay on until then, rather than arriving right at peak time.

The passing train — a surprise treat for kids

The vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat tourist railway runs right below the restaurant. For adults it's a view; for kids, a real train passing a few dozen meters away, its whistle echoing across the valley, is often what they remember most from the whole trip.

Trains pass the restaurant within the 16:30 – 21:25 window — you'll find train-spotting tips on our Da Lat train-view BBQ page. If you want your child to see a train, aim to arrive about 30 minutes before the train window and book ahead to get a spot with nothing blocking the view.

A birthday for your little one — free setup

If your trip falls on your child's birthday, we decorate the party table completely free of charge: a greeting board and decorative flowers on the table. There's no decoration fee. Since we need to prepare in advance, we take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date and refund it to you after your meal — regular table bookings need no deposit. Just add a note when you book or let us know 1 day ahead via WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070; details are on our birthday page.

A bonus: the backdrop comes ready-made — the valley at sunset, then the greenhouse sea of stars after dark — so your child's birthday photos need no extra backdrop.

Cost for a family of 4

Spending is usually 95,000–300,000 VND per person, VAT included. For a family of 4 that works out to roughly 380,000–1,200,000 VND — a figure based on per-person spending, not the price of a fixed set meal. A single hotpot alone starts at 300,000 VND (Chicken Hotpot with É Leaves), so a meal with grilled dishes, hotpot and drinks is unlikely to come in at the bottom of this range. We serve à la carte only, with no combos or fixed set menus, so your family pays only for what you order.

There's no surcharge for view tables: train-view, sunset-view and greenhouse-view tables all cost the same. We accept cash and bank transfer.

Directions and parking

We're at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward (formerly Ward 11, the Trai Mat area), about 7 km, roughly a 20-minute drive from central Da Lat — Da Lat Market and Xuan Huong Lake — heading towards Trai Mat.

With small children a car is more convenient, and we have free parking for both cars and motorbikes right at the restaurant. At weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away — one more reason to come early, from 15:00. Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.

A few things to consider before you go

Families with small children are welcome, but there are a few things parents should know in advance: the grills use charcoal, so there's always fire and a hot grill plate; the restaurant sits right beside a working railway; and this is a restaurant space, with no dedicated play area for children.

To be fair, here are a few things parents should know in advance:

  • The grills use charcoal — there's always fire and a hot grill plate. For children under 3, seat them on the inner side, away from the grill.
  • The restaurant sits right beside a working railway. The open-air railing rows look straight down onto the track — with little ones, don't let them climb the railing or run towards the track, especially when a train is about to pass.
  • There's no dedicated play area for children. The space is large and airy, but it's a restaurant, not a kids' zone.
  • Da Lat evenings are cold, especially at the outdoor seats. Bring an extra warm layer for your child, even though we have blankets.
  • The road back at night through the Trai Mat area is quiet and dark. If your family is riding motorbikes, head back before 21:00.

When you book, add the note “with small children” so we can seat you in an open spot away from the walkways, where it's easy for our staff to help keep an eye on your child.
