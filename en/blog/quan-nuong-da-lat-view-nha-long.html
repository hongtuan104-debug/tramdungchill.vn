What is Da Lat's greenhouse "sea of stars"?

The "nhà lồng" are Da Lat's greenhouses, where local farmers grow flowers and vegetables, and they blanket many of the valleys around the city. Every evening the lights inside them come on — and seen from above, the whole valley turns into a glittering "sea of stars" in alternating white and yellow. It is something very particular to Da Lat, and to take it all in you need a high, open vantage point looking straight down into the valley.

Trạm Dừng Chill has this kind of vantage point: the restaurant sits high on a hill with a 180° view over the greenhouse valley, so you can grill BBQ at your table while the whole valley lights up below.

What makes the view here special is not just the sea of stars but a sequence of 3 scenes, one after another from afternoon into night: the valley sunset and the vintage train passing by (both starting from around 16:30) → the greenhouse sea of stars lighting up. The restaurant is open 15:00–23:00 daily, so you can come early and catch all three.

When trains pass the restaurant

The vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat railway (a roughly 7 km stretch still running tourist trains, part of a line built by the French) runs right below the restaurant. Trains pass Trạm Dừng Chill during this window:

The vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat train passes the restaurant within the 16:30 – 21:25 window.

Golden window: arrive around 16:00 to be there when the trains start passing the restaurant (16:30), then stay on for the sunset. For more on when trains pass the restaurant, see our BBQ with a train view page.

A quick note on bookings: between 17:30 and 19:00 we're usually full and don't take new reservations — if you want to be sure of a table, come earlier or choose 19:30 onwards (the sea of stars is still dazzling by then).

Which season is best for the sea of stars?

Da Lat's greenhouses are lit all year round. The clearest views come in the dry season (roughly November to April), when there's less cloud and mist. In the rainy season, fog may hide part of the valley, but it creates its own dreamy "lights in the mist" effect. Whatever the season, bring a jacket — Da Lat evenings get quite cold.

How it compares with other Da Lat valley viewpoints

Da Lat has many kinds of spots for watching the sunset and the valleys, each with its own strengths. A neutral comparison:

  • High lookouts (hills, mountaintops): you get a panorama of the city or the plateau in the late afternoon, but they're usually just for looking — no food or drink on site, and some involve a steep climb.
  • Cafés / viewpoints in the city center: convenient and nice for sipping a drink, but the view is often blocked by buildings and rarely takes in the greenhouse valleys.
  • Trạm Dừng Chill: a 180° view of the greenhouse valley + sunset + train, BBQ grilled right at your table, open 15:00–23:00 — sightseeing and a full meal in one place.

The difference at Trạm Dừng Chill is that you don't have to choose between "the view" and "good food" — the three views unfold one after another while you enjoy your BBQ.

Frequently asked questions

What time do Da Lat's greenhouses light up for the sea of stars?

The greenhouses are clearly lit from around 18:30 onwards and stay bright all evening. At Trạm Dừng Chill (high on a hill overlooking the valley), you can watch the sea of stars until we close at 23:00.

Where is the best place to see the greenhouse sea of stars?

It's best from a high point looking straight down into the valley. Trạm Dừng Chill (111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward) has a 180° view over the greenhouse valley, so you can grill BBQ and take in the view at the same time. You can book a view table on our website.

What time does the vintage train pass the restaurant?

Da Lat – Trai Mat trains pass the restaurant between 16:30 and 21:25. Find out more on our Da Lat train-spotting page.

What's the best season for the Da Lat greenhouse view?

The dry season (roughly November to April) has clear skies and little cloud, so the view is sharpest. The rainy season brings fog and its own dreamy 'lights in the mist' effect. The greenhouses are lit all year round.

What time should I arrive to see the sunset, the train and the sea of stars?

Aim to be there around 16:00. You'll catch the trains from when they start passing the restaurant (16:30), watch the sunset (from around 16:30), then stay until the greenhouses light up from ~18:30.

How do I book a good view table at Trạm Dừng Chill?

Book through the booking form on our website; we'll confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within about 15 minutes (during opening hours). Avoid 17:30–19:00 as we're usually full; choose an earlier time or from 19:30. WhatsApp: +84 989 765 070.

Is there car parking?

Trạm Dừng Chill has free parking for both motorbikes and cars right at the restaurant. We're at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. At weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away. We open at 15:00, so arriving early makes parking easier; Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.
