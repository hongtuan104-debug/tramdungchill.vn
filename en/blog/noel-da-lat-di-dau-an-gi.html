Christmas in Da Lat: Where to Go, What to Eat and a Christmas Eve (24/12) Plan From Afternoon to Evening

Christmas in Da Lat: where to go and what to eat? Here's a plan for Christmas Eve (24/12): churches in the afternoon, the sunset and the vintage train at Trạm Dừng Chill, then an evening of BBQ as the greenhouses light up, fireworks and photos at the Christmas train area.

TL;DR: For Christmas in Da Lat on the night of 24/12, visit the churches early in the afternoon to beat the crowds, head up to Trạm Dừng Chill (about 7 km from the center) around 16:00 for the sunset and the vintage train, then have a grill-at-your-table BBQ dinner as the greenhouses light up. This Christmas season we have fireworks every evening; the Christmas train area is open to everyone for free photos through 31/12/2026, and there's no surcharge on 24/12.

Christmas Eve in Da Lat is one of the busiest nights of the year. The city center fills up from early evening, and traffic around the churches crawls along. The most comfortable approach is to split the evening in two: spend the first half somewhere open, with a view and a hot meal; only head back into town for the second half if you still have the energy to keep strolling. The plan below is our suggestion as the restaurant team, based on what guests ask us every Christmas season.

What's on for Christmas in Da Lat?

For Christmas in Da Lat, especially on the night of 24/12, there are three main kinds of places to go: churches decorated with nativity scenes and Christmas lights, the night market and Lam Vien Square in the center, and open-air restaurants looking out over the valley on the edge of the city.

  • Da Lat Cathedral (known locally as the Rooster Church, 13 Tran Phu Street) is the busiest spot on Christmas Eve. According to Wikipedia, it is the largest church in Da Lat and one of the oldest buildings the French left behind in the city.
  • Domaine de Marie Church (1 Ngo Quyen Street) has spacious grounds and is easier to photograph by day than in the evening.
  • The night market and Lam Vien Square get busy from early evening, good for a stroll and some street snacks after your main meal.
  • Open-air BBQ restaurants on the edge of town like Trạm Dừng Chill: airy, with a sunset, greenhouses lighting up and a vintage train passing by.

If you'd like to attend Christmas Eve Mass, ask the church directly for the service times, as the schedule can change from year to year and the area around the church is usually very crowded before Mass begins.

Christmas in Da Lat: a 24/12 plan from afternoon to evening

The simplest plan for 24/12 is to visit the churches early in the afternoon, head up to the restaurant for dinner before the peak hours, and only go back into town once the center has thinned out.

13:30–15:00Stroll around Da Lat Cathedral or Domaine de Marie while it's still light, and photograph the nativity scenes and Christmas trees.
15:00–16:30Head up to Trạm Dừng Chill (we open at 15:00), photograph the Christmas train area while the sun is still out, and pick a table overlooking the valley.

From around 18:30The greenhouses in the valley below light up, and the Christmas train area glows.

Between 17:30 and 19:00 we're usually full, so book and arrive before then if you want to choose your seat. Actual train times may change with the operating schedule. On Christmas Eve the center often has traffic jams, so allow extra time for the roughly 7 km trip from the center up to the restaurant.

Christmas in Da Lat: what to eat to stay warm?

Christmas evenings in Da Lat are cold, so hot food you can eat slowly suits them best: a grill-at-your-table BBQ, a hotpot in the middle and a few cups of ginger tea to warm your hands.

Trạm Dừng Chill doesn't do a special Christmas menu. You order dishes individually from the menu of more than 70 dishes; there are no combos or fixed sets for individual diners, so you only pay for what you order. A few dishes that suit cold evenings:

  • Grilled dishes: Kobe Beef with Salted Egg Cheese, Enoki Beef Rolls, Grilled Shrimp with Chili Salt.
  • Hotpot: Chicken Hotpot with É Leaves or Sturgeon Hotpot for groups who want a hot pot in the middle of the table.
  • Warm drinks: Ginger Tea with Red Dates & Honey, Chrysanthemum Tea with Red Dates.

Typical spending is 150,000–400,000 VND per person, VAT included; for a group of 4 that comes to about 600,000–1,600,000 VND. On 24/12 there is no surcharge, and view tables carry no surcharge either. If you feel cold sitting outdoors, just tell the staff: we have blankets and charcoal heaters we can bring right to your table.

Fireworks and the Christmas train area at Trạm Dừng Chill

This Christmas season, Trạm Dừng Chill has fireworks every evening, and the snow-covered Christmas train area is open to everyone for free photos through 31/12/2026.

The Christmas area is outdoors, with a display locomotive and carriages outlined in yellow lights, a track, rows of snow-covered pines and a corner with a falling-snow effect. Anyone can come in to take photos — you don't have to eat or drink here. On 24/12 this area is at its busiest, so take your photos early, at dusk, then return to your table for dinner. The Christmas train display is only a decoration; the real vintage train runs on the track below the restaurant. Photos and detailed photo angles are in our article Christmas in Da Lat 2026: Snow Photo Spot and a Christmas Train.

How to book for 24/12 without missing out on a table

On 24/12, book early, arrive before 17:30 and say clearly in the notes whether you'd like to sit near the Christmas area or right by the railing to watch the train.

  • Booking: book directly on our website or call +84 989 765 070. We confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within about 15 minutes if you book during opening hours (15:00–23:00); bookings made outside those hours are confirmed once we open.
  • Deposit: regular tables don't need a deposit; for groups of more than 10, our staff will tell you the deposit amount when confirming.
  • Parking: free parking for motorbikes and cars; at peak times you may have to park about 100–200 m further away.
  • Address: 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, about 7 km from the center. See directions with a video guide.

What to wear for Christmas in Da Lat?

For Christmas Eve in Da Lat, dress in layers: a thermal base layer underneath, a thick jacket on top, plus a scarf and closed shoes, because sitting outdoors after sunset is much colder than during the day.

December in Da Lat is the start of the dry season, so there's less rain than in the middle of the year, but nights bring mist and wind from the valley. If you're riding a motorbike back to town after dinner, bring gloves and ride slowly: the road through the Trai Mat area is quite quiet and dark at night. If you're caught in a sudden shower, we give every guest a raincoat to take home.

A Christmas Eve in Da Lat doesn't have to cover every spot. An afternoon at a church, a hot dinner by the valley, a few photos in the snow and a fireworks show are enough to remember for a long time.

Frequently asked questions

Where should you go for Christmas in Da Lat?

On 24/12, you can stroll around Da Lat Cathedral (the Rooster Church) or Domaine de Marie in the afternoon; from around 16:00, head up to Trạm Dừng Chill at 111 Huynh Tan Phat for the sunset, the vintage train, the greenhouses lighting up and photos at the Christmas train area, then go back to the night market if you want to keep exploring.

What should you eat to stay warm on Christmas Eve in Da Lat?

Grill-at-your-table BBQ and a hot hotpot suit the cold weather. At Trạm Dừng Chill, you order individual dishes from a menu of more than 70 dishes, with typical spending of 150,000–400,000 VND per person, VAT included.

Is there an extra charge for Christmas Eve dinner at Trạm Dừng Chill?

No. On 24/12 we keep our regular prices and there's no surcharge for view tables.

Does Trạm Dừng Chill have fireworks during the Christmas season?

Yes. This Christmas season, we have fireworks every evening. The Christmas train area is open to everyone for free photos through 31/12/2026.

Do I need to book ahead for 24/12?

Booking ahead is a good idea, as we're usually full between 17:30 and 19:00. Book on our website or call +84 989 765 070; regular tables don't need a deposit.

How far is Trạm Dừng Chill from central Da Lat?

About 7 km, at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward. We have free parking for motorbikes and cars.
