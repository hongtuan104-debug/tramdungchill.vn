Da Lat Hotpot & BBQ in the Cold Season — Build Your Own Warming Meal, with Misty and Train Views

Where to eat hotpot and BBQ in Da Lat's cold season? Trạm Dừng Chill — warm charcoal grills, steaming hotpots, views of the misty valley & the train, rated 4.8/5 from 7,344 Google reviews.

In Da Lat's cold season, the best way to enjoy hotpot and BBQ is to sit by a glowing charcoal grill while sipping from a steaming hotpot in the mist. Tiệm Nướng Trạm Dừng Chill (111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward) delivers that whole feeling: outdoor tables with a 180° valley view, the vintage train running right below the restaurant, and a 4.8/5 rating from 7,344 reviews on Google Maps (as of 18/10/2026). Why do hotpot + BBQ suit Da Lat's cold season?

Unlike Saigon, which is hot all year round, Da Lat in its cold season (roughly December to February, when the average nighttime low is only about 11–13°C according to the climate table on Wikipedia) is chilly by day and quite cold at night, so you can sit outdoors comfortably all evening. A glowing charcoal grill radiates heat on one side, a steaming hotpot on the other — warming you inside and out. The smell of grilling meat mingling with the crisp air, and the white mist blanketing the valley, make for an experience you only get in a mountain town.

Ordering both hotpot and BBQ also keeps the whole group happy: BBQ lovers can keep flipping meat on the grill, while those who prefer something hot and brothy can dip into the hotpot. Especially for big groups, a hotpot in the middle of the table means there's always something hot to pick at while you wait for the next round off the grill.

3 cold-season "specialty" views at Trạm Dừng Chill

  • Train view: the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat railway (a roughly 7 km stretch that still runs tourist trains, part of a line built by the French) runs right below the restaurant. Grilling as the train whistle sounds is a moment all its own.
  • Valley sunset: golden afternoon light from 15:00, sunset from around 16:30; the 180° view bathes the whole greenhouse valley in gold.
  • The greenhouse "sea of stars": around 18:30, thousands of greenhouses light up at once, glowing through the mist like a sea of stars down in the valley.

Tips for staying warm all evening at a cold-season hotpot-and-BBQ dinner in Da Lat

The most effective way to stay warm is to order the hotpot first and grill while you wait for the broth to boil, so there's always something hot on the table. If it gets too cold sitting outside, just tell the staff — the restaurant has blankets and charcoal heaters it can bring right to your table, and a covered area for rainy days.

  • Order the hotpot first so the broth has time to boil, and grill while you wait — eating and staying warm at the same time.
  • Wear a thick jacket, a scarf and closed shoes, because sitting outdoors at night gets quite cold; if it's too chilly, tell the staff — the restaurant has blankets and charcoal heaters it can bring to your table, and a covered area for rainy days. Bring some wipes too, since BBQ + hotpot smells tend to cling.
  • Arrive around 16:00 to get a table before the sunset and the train window both begin (16:30).
  • Go for hot drinks (herbal tea, warm fruit tea) for double the warmth.
  • On weekends or holidays, book ahead, because the best time slots fill up very fast.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I eat hotpot and BBQ with a beautiful view in Da Lat's cold season?

Trạm Dừng Chill (111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward) is a good choice if you want to sit outdoors: outdoor tables with a 180° valley view, the vintage train running right below the restaurant, and the greenhouse 'sea of stars' at 18:30. Book on the restaurant's homepage.

What kinds of hotpot does Trạm Dừng Chill serve?

The restaurant has 3 kinds of hotpot, priced per pot: Chicken Hotpot with É Leaves at 300,000 VND/pot (a Da Lat specialty), Seafood Hotpot at 320,000 VND/pot and Sturgeon Hotpot at 320,000 VND/pot. You can pair them with best-selling grilled dishes at the same table. See the menu page for details.

What should I wear for a cold-season hotpot-and-BBQ dinner?

Wear a thick jacket, a scarf and closed shoes, because sitting outdoors at night gets quite cold. Bring some wipes too, as BBQ and hotpot smells tend to cling — but that's Da Lat's 'scent of memories'.

What time should I arrive to enjoy BBQ and watch the train?

Arrive around 16:00 to catch the trains from when they start passing the restaurant (16:30), then stay on for the sunset. Note that the restaurant is usually full between 17:30 and 19:00; come earlier or from 19:30. See the train times on the Da Lat train-spotting page.

How much does hotpot and BBQ at Trạm Dừng Chill cost per person?

Expect to spend about 150,000–400,000 VND per person, depending on what you order. Prices include VAT and may change with the season; from the 2nd to the 8th day of Tết (Lunar New Year) there is a 10% surcharge.

How do I book a table for a cold-season hotpot-and-BBQ dinner?

Fill in the booking form on the restaurant's website and we'll confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within about 15 minutes (during opening hours), or call us directly at +84 989 765 070. On weekends and holidays, book early, as the best time slots fill up fast.

Is there car parking?

Trạm Dừng Chill has free parking for both motorbikes and cars right at the restaurant. We're at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. At weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away. We open at 15:00, so arriving early makes parking easier; Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.
