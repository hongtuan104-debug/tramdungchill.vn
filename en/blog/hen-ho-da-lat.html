Why is Trạm Dừng Chill a good fit for a date night in Da Lat?

Many restaurants in Da Lat excel at one kind of scenery — a lake view, a hill view, or a cozy indoor space. Trạm Dừng Chill offers something different: three romantic moments one after another in the same evening, all seen from your own grill table.

  • Valley sunset: golden afternoon light from 15:00, with the sunset settling in from around 16:30; a 180° view sweeping down over the valley and the greenhouse gardens — the best time for couple photos.
  • A vintage train right below the restaurant: the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat railway (a roughly 7 km section that still runs tourist trains, part of a line built by the French) passes underneath. Hearing the train whistle echo up while you're grilling is a feeling unique to this mountain town.
  • The greenhouse "sea of stars": around 18:30, thousands of greenhouses in the valley switch on their lights at once, twinkling like a sea of stars — the most romantic moment of the day.

The outdoor tables are airy and spaced just far enough apart to give two people room for a private conversation by the warm grill in the cool mountain air. It's a "chill but classy" kind of date: grilling for each other while taking in the view, without the stiffness of a closed-in restaurant.

Date, birthday and anniversary setups — free

If you'd like to surprise your partner, you can ask us to decorate your table — this is included free when you book ahead (we take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date and refund it after your meal). Just tell us the occasion (date, birthday, anniversary) when you book so we can prepare. It's also a popular choice for occasions like proposals & dates or birthday parties at the restaurant.

Tips for a smooth surprise: book at least 1 day ahead (earlier for weekends and holidays), spell out your setup requests, and arrive 15–20 minutes before your partner to check that the table is ready.

Quick comparison of BBQ spots for couples in Da Lat

Da Lat has many kinds of BBQ spots suited to a date, each with its own strength. Here are a few suggestions by type, so you can see how Trạm Dừng Chill is different:

  • Lakeside BBQ restaurants: a relaxing lakeside setting, great for gazing at the water — the highlight is usually a single, still scene.
  • Hillside / camping-style BBQ spots: airy, looking out over the city or the hills at night — the experience leans towards outdoor camping.
  • Cozy indoor BBQ restaurants: sheltered from the wind and private, good for very cold days — but there's no scenery to enjoy.
  • Trạm Dừng Chill: brings all three moments together in one evening — the sunset, the vintage train passing below, then the greenhouse sea of stars lighting up — plus outdoor tables for two, signature dishes, a free setup and clear pricing of 150,000–400,000 VND per person (4.8★, over 7,344 reviews).

Trạm Dừng Chill's real advantage is this: few restaurants in Da Lat bring all three view moments together in one evening, with signature dishes, a free setup and clear pricing on top. If the two of you put "views + experience" first for your date night, this is an easy place to score points.

Special-occasion date nights: Valentine's Day, 8/3 (Women's Day), anniversaries

On occasions like Valentine's Day or 8/3 (Women's Day), demand for good view tables rises sharply, so if you're planning for a special day, book earlier than usual and tell us your setup requests in advance so we have time to prepare. A sensible order for two is still one signature grilled beef dish, some seafood, a hotpot to keep warm and drinks — we serve à la carte, so you can balance it yourself — just enough for two to linger over the whole sunset and then the sea of stars lighting up.

Frequently asked questions

Does Trạm Dừng Chill have private tables for 2?

What time should I arrive to see both the sunset and the train?

Arrive around 16:00 to be in time for when trains start passing the restaurant (16:30), then stay for the sunset (from around 16:30). Around 18:30 the greenhouse sea of stars lights up.

Do you offer a romantic table setup (decorative flowers, decorations) for special occasions?

Where is Trạm Dừng Chill and when is it open?

We're at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, Lam Dong (formerly Ward 11), open 15:00–23:00 every day. Contact us by WhatsApp/phone at +84 989 765 070.

Is there car parking?

Trạm Dừng Chill has free parking for both motorbikes and cars right at the restaurant. We open at 15:00, so arriving early makes parking easier; Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.
