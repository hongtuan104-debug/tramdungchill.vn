Da Lat 3-Day, 2-Night Itinerary — Everything from A to Z

A detailed 3-day, 2-night Da Lat itinerary broken down by time of day: specialties in the morning, coffee in the afternoon, and BBQ in the evening with the sunset + vintage train at Trạm Dừng Chill.

The most sensible way to plan 3 days and 2 nights in Da Lat is by time of day: specialty food in the morning, coffee with a hillside view in the afternoon, and the late afternoon and evening of the first day set aside for a BBQ dinner with a view. A food highlight many visitors choose is Tiệm Nướng Trạm Dừng Chill — where you can grill while watching the valley sunset and the vintage railway running right below the restaurant, rated 4.8/5 from 7,344 reviews on Google Maps (as of 18/10/2026).

3-day, 2-night itinerary at a glance

Da Lat is at its best when you take it slow. 3 days and 2 nights is the ideal amount of time to explore the center and get out into the countryside without rushing. Below is a day-by-day itinerary, with real places to eat to help you plan.

Day 1 — Central Da Lat & the highlight dinner

Morning: a proper highland start

Start the day with bánh mì xíu mại — Da Lat's legendary breakfast, with a rich sauce and soft, tender meatballs. Then stop by a café with a hillside view, order a hot phin-filtered Arabica and watch the mist lift in the early sunshine. It's a moment of calm that belongs to the misty city alone.

Midday: stroll the center, eat street food

Visit the sights in the center and take photos at the city flower garden. For lunch, try bún bò Huế (Da Lat has a large community from Hue, so it tastes authentic), cơm tấm (broken rice) with charcoal-grilled pork ribs, or mì Quảng with a rich broth.

Afternoon - Evening: BBQ with 3 views at Trạm Dừng Chill

This is the highlight of the whole trip. Trạm Dừng Chill is open 15:00–23:00 at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward. Aim to arrive around 16:00: you'll catch the trains from when they start passing the restaurant (16:30), then stay on to watch the 180° sunset over the valley (from around 16:30), and around 18:30 thousands of greenhouses light up all at once to create a shimmering "greenhouse sea of stars". If you'd like to compare, right next door is Xóm Lèo (at number 113, our sister restaurant), which also serves BBQ overlooking the greenhouse valley.

What makes it special is the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat railway (a roughly 7 km stretch that still runs tourist trains, part of a line built by the French) passing right below the restaurant. Grilling while you hear the train whistle is a rare experience. Time your visit to the window when trains pass the restaurant so you're ready for photos:

The vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat train passes the restaurant within the 16:30 – 21:25 window.

Food-wise, Trạm Dừng Chill is a grill-at-your-table BBQ restaurant that also serves hotpot, seafood, sides and drinks — over 70 à la carte items, at 95,000–300,000 VND per person, VAT included. A few best sellers: Enoki Beef Rolls (137,000 VND), Salt & Pepper Beef Belly (155,000 VND), and the signature Kobe Beef with Salted Egg Cheese at 209,000 VND. If you love seafood, there's Shaking Sturgeon (165,000 VND), Stuffed Snails (165,000 VND) and Salted Egg Shrimp (160,000 VND). See the full menu.

Note: Trạm Dừng Chill is usually full between 17:30 and 19:00, so it doesn't take new bookings with arrival times in that window — choose an arrival time before 17:30 or from 19:30. For more on timing the trains, see our Da Lat train-spotting article.

Day 2 — The outskirts, nature & the night market

Spend the morning on Da Lat chicken phở — crystal-clear broth and firm free-range chicken — then head out to explore the outskirts: valleys, the Cau Dat tea hills, or some light trekking. For lunch, be sure to try chicken hotpot with é leaves, free-range chicken cooked with fragrant é leaves (Trạm Dừng Chill also serves Chicken Hotpot with É Leaves at 300,000 VND a pot, but the restaurant opens at 15:00, so you can only have it for a late-afternoon or evening meal).

Relax in the afternoon at a café overlooking the pine forest. In the evening, stroll Da Lat Night Market for grilled rice paper ("Da Lat pizza"), snails and hot soy milk — and end the day with a warm cup of soy milk in the cool night air.

Day 3 — Coffee and shopping before heading home

Take it easy in the morning with hot, crispy bánh căn dipped in sweet fish sauce, plus soy milk. For lunch, have bún bò Huế or go back for more chicken hotpot with é leaves. Before leaving the city, stop by Da Lat Market for specialties: candied strawberries, Arabica coffee, artichoke tea, wine. Ask the price first so you don't get overcharged.

Estimated costs (for reference)

A 3-day, 2-night trip to Da Lat usually comes to about 1.8–3.5 million VND per person, not counting intercity bus or plane tickets. The figure drops considerably with two or more people, since you can split the room and the bike rental.

Cutting it down to 2 days, 1 night?

If you only have a weekend, you can still get the full experience: bánh mì xíu mại + coffee in the morning, chicken hotpot with é leaves + avocado ice cream at lunch, BBQ at Trạm Dừng Chill in the late afternoon and evening, then ride back to the center to stroll the night market. The next day, have a leisurely breakfast and buy specialties before heading home. The total cost is much lower.

Why should dinner on Day 1 be at Trạm Dừng Chill?

Compared with just eating BBQ, the restaurant's real advantage is a "3-in-1" experience that few places offer:

  • Vintage train view — the Da Lat – Trai Mat line runs right below the restaurant, something most BBQ restaurants in the city don't have.
  • 180° valley sunset (golden afternoon light from 15:00, sunset from around 16:30) and the greenhouse "sea of stars" from around 18:30.
  • Free birthday / anniversary setup — party table decoration included at no charge (200,000 VND deposit, refunded after your meal), great for groups celebrating a special occasion.
  • Transparent pricing — a clearly priced menu, 95,000–300,000 VND per person, VAT included.
  • Genuine reviews — 4.8 stars from over 7,344 reviews on Google.

If your trip includes a special occasion, have a look at birthday parties, dates and proposals or team building for a setup that suits you.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 3-day, 2-night trip to Da Lat cost?

About 1.8–3.5 million VND per person for 3 days and 2 nights, not counting intercity travel. How it adds up: 2 nights in a double room at 300,000–600,000 VND/room/night, food and drink at 200,000–400,000 VND/person/day, entrance tickets at 100,000–200,000 VND/person/day, and motorbike rental at 100,000–150,000 VND/bike/day for 3 days, assuming one person pays for the whole room and bike. Groups sharing rooms and bikes will spend less; on holidays and weekends, room rates are usually higher.

What should I eat and where should I go in Da Lat in the evening?

A combination many visitors choose: arrive at Trạm Dừng Chill from 16:00 for BBQ with the sunset and the vintage train, stay until around 18:30 for the greenhouse sea of stars, then ride back to the center to snack your way through the night market. The restaurant is open 15:00–23:00.

What time does the vintage train pass Trạm Dừng Chill?

Trains pass the restaurant between 16:30 and 21:25. More on our Da Lat train-spotting page.

What time should I arrive at Trạm Dừng Chill to get a good seat?

Arrive around 16:00 to catch the trains from when they start passing the restaurant (16:30), then stay on for the sunset. Note that the restaurant is usually full between 17:30 and 19:00 and doesn't take new bookings then, so come earlier or choose 19:30 onwards, and book ahead on the website.

How many days should I spend in Da Lat?

Ideally 3 days and 2 nights, so you can explore both the center and the outskirts and eat at a relaxed pace. If you only have a weekend, 2 days and 1 night is still enough to try the main specialties and enjoy a BBQ dinner with a view.

What are Trạm Dừng Chill's standout dishes, and how much do they cost?

The restaurant has over 70 dishes, at 95,000–300,000 VND per person. The signature dish is Kobe Beef with Salted Egg Cheese (209,000 VND); best sellers include Enoki Beef Rolls (137,000 VND) and Shaking Sturgeon (165,000 VND). See the full menu.

Is there car parking?

Trạm Dừng Chill has free parking for both motorbikes and cars right at the restaurant. We're at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. At weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away. We open at 15:00, so arriving early makes parking easier; Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.
