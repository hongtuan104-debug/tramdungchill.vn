Which view do you want?
Each view has its own row of tables

Trạm Dừng Chill is an open-air grill house and the tables are arranged in rows. Every table in the same row shares the same view — no need to fight for the first table.

You want the train right below you

The open-air railing row sits closest to the track and is the most requested row — on weekends and in high season it is booked out 2–4 weeks ahead. Booking late? Take row X: also open-air railing, train still very close. Big group? Row G is right next door, shares the same train view, and seats everyone in one line.

You want the sunset and the greenhouse lights

The covered railing row sits higher up with the full panorama: golden afternoon light from 15:00, sunset from around 16:30, then from about 18:30 the whole greenhouse valley lights up. Row B next to it faces the same way. Both rows are roofed, so they are the comfortable pick on a rainy or cold Da Lat evening.

You want all three from one seat

Area K is our newest open-air railing row and takes in the sunset, the greenhouses and the train. Rows away from the track sit right beside our own photo corners: the train check-in spots and the windmill.

No surcharge for view tables — every row costs the same. Just name the row you want when you book and our staff can hold it faster.

Where is Trạm Dừng Chill, and how far is it from central Da Lat?

We are at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, Lam Dong (formerly Ward 11, the Trai Mat area). It is about 7 km from central Da Lat — Da Lat Market and Xuan Huong Lake — roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.

What time does the restaurant open?

We open 15:00 to 23:00 every day, including weekends and public holidays. Arriving at 15:00 lets you pick a table before the sunset starts around 16:30; the vintage train passes between 16:30 and 21:25 and the greenhouses light up around 18:30.

How much does a meal cost per person?

From 150,000 VND to 400,000 VND per person, VAT included. For a group of 4, that works out to roughly 600,000 – 1,600,000 VND. The menu has over 70 à la carte items, each clearly priced, including BBQ, hotpot, seafood and drinks. See the detailed price list by category →

How do I book a table in advance?

Book through the form right on this page, or call or WhatsApp us at +84 989 765 070. During opening hours (15:00–23:00), we confirm by WhatsApp or SMS within 15 minutes; bookings made outside those hours are confirmed once we open. Book ahead to get a table with a sunset view or a view of the greenhouses lighting up.

Do I need to pay a deposit to book a table?

A regular booking needs no deposit — just message or call us to reserve and we'll hold your table. We only ask for a deposit in two cases where we need to prepare ahead: first, when you'd like us to decorate for a birthday or party — the decoration is free, and we take a 200,000 VND deposit that is refunded after your meal; second, for groups of more than 10. Our staff will tell you the deposit amount and how to transfer it when they confirm your booking by WhatsApp or SMS.

Is there car parking?

Yes, free parking for both motorbikes and cars. At weekend peak hours you may need to park about 100–200m further away. We open at 15:00, so arriving early makes parking easier.

Is seating outdoors or indoors? What if it rains?

We have outdoor tables with a 180° valley view plus a large covered area that still looks out over the valley and the glowing greenhouses, so rain is not a problem. When it rains we hand out free raincoats; on cold nights we provide blankets and charcoal heaters. View tables carry no surcharge.

Is it comfortable to come with small children?

Yes. Our staff keep an eye on little ones so parents can enjoy the meal — "come and eat, we will look after the kids" — but this is help from our serving staff, not a childcare service, so parents stay responsible for their children. The restaurant sits right beside a working railway, so keep children seated when a train passes; the covered railing row and row B are roofed and less windy, a good fit for families.

Do you take large groups or team-building events?

Yes. We host groups of 20 to 100 guests for team building and company birthday parties. Let us know 2 days ahead via WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 so we can arrange tables and suggest dishes for your group. We serve à la carte only — no combos or fixed set menus — so your group pays only for what it orders. See our team-building tips →

Do you set up birthdays and anniversaries?

Yes, and it's free: a greeting board and a party table decorated with decorative flowers, with no decoration fee. We take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date and refund it after your meal. Just add a note when you book, or let us know 1 day ahead via WhatsApp. See how we set up birthdays →

What should I order at Trạm Dừng Chill?

Our signature dish is the grilled beef slab with salted egg cheese — Kobe beef at 209,000 VND or Okachi beef at 229,000 VND — a best seller guests talk about a lot. Grilled seafood (grilled shrimp with chili salt, satay-marinated squid, grilled octopus, grilled sturgeon) and our 3 hotpots (chicken with é leaves, seafood, sturgeon) are also very popular. See the list of dishes worth ordering →

Is there an English menu?

Yes. The menu is translated into English for international guests, and our staff speak basic English. View the menu online at tramdungchill.vn/menu.html. A guide for international guests →

Can I bring my pet?

Small pets such as dogs and cats are welcome in the outdoor area. We only ask that you keep them on a leash and clean up after them.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and bank transfer.
