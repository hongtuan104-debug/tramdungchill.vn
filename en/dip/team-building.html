Quick answers

Frequently asked questions
about company parties & team building

How many guests can you host per group?

From 20 to 100 guests for team building and company birthday parties. We have a small stage and photo spots for groups.

How far in advance should I let you know?

Let us know 2 days ahead via WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 so we can arrange tables and suggest dishes for your group. Groups of more than 10 pay a deposit; our staff will tell you the amount when confirming.

How much does it cost per person?

From 95,000 – 300,000 VND per person, VAT included, depending on how much your group orders. Call +84 989 765 070 and we'll suggest dishes to suit your headcount and budget.

How should a large group order?

We serve à la carte only, with no combos or fixed set menus — your group pays only for what it orders, never for food it doesn't eat. Message us your headcount and budget in advance, and our staff will suggest a spread from over 70 dishes: grill-at-your-table BBQ, hotpot, seafood and drinks.

Where can a large group park?

We have a large parking area for motorbikes and cars. At weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away — large groups should let us know in advance so we can make arrangements.

Where is the restaurant and what are the opening hours?

111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, Lam Dong, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. Open 15:00 – 23:00 every day, including weekends and public holidays. We accept cash and bank transfer.
