How much does a proposal with us cost?

95,000 – 300,000 VND/person

VAT included — for two people, that works out to roughly 190,000 – 600,000 VND. The proposal setup itself: 0 VND.

The proposal board, table decoration and attentive service are our gift and are not added to your bill. We serve à la carte only, with no combos or fixed set menus, so you pay only for what you order.

  • No surcharge for view tables — sunset-view seats cost the same as every other table.
  • No surcharge on Valentine's Day or the night of 24/12 — prices stay the same as on any other day. The exception is Tết (Lunar New Year): from the 2nd to the 8th day of Tết, we add a 10% surcharge.
  • 200,000 VND deposit, refunded after your meal — only when you confirm a setup date, since we need to prepare in advance. Regular table bookings need no deposit.
  • Free parking for cars and motorbikes.

To pin down the cost of your own concept, call or message us on WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 — we're open 15:00 – 23:00 every day.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions
about dates & proposals

Is there a charge for the proposal setup?

The setup is free, with no surcharge — it includes a "Marry Me?" board or your own personal message, table decoration, a great view table and help capturing the moment. Since we need to prepare in advance, we take a 200,000 VND deposit when you confirm the date and refund it after your meal. Regular table bookings need no deposit.

Can you help keep it a secret?

Yes. Message us privately on WhatsApp at +84 989 765 070 with your concept and a request to keep it secret — we'll plan everything without letting your partner find out.

How far in advance should I let you know?

Giving us 1 day's notice via WhatsApp is enough for a basic setup. For a more elaborate proposal concept, message us earlier so we can prepare properly.

What's the most romantic time to come?

The most romantic stretch runs from late afternoon until the greenhouses light up: golden light over the valley from 15:00, sunset from around 16:30 (you'll need clear skies), then from around 18:30 thousands of greenhouses light up like a sea of stars. After 19:30 the crowd thins out and it's quieter.

How much does a meal for two cost?

From 95,000 – 300,000 VND per person, VAT included, so for two people that works out to roughly 190,000 – 600,000 VND. No surcharge for view tables, and the setup is free of charge.

Where is the restaurant, and where can I park?

111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, Lam Dong, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. Open 15:00 – 23:00 every day, including weekends and public holidays. Free parking for motorbikes and cars; at weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away. We accept cash and bank transfer.
