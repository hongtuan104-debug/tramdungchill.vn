Why go “train-spotting” with us
at least 1 time in your life?

Because at Trạm Dừng Chill, the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat train runs right below the restaurant's railing: “train-spotting” means timing your visit to watch and film the train as it passes, within the window when trains pass the restaurant, 16:30 – 21:25. There is more than one place to watch from: the open-air railing row right beside the track is where you see the train closest; the covered railing row higher up looks out over both the sunset and the greenhouse valley. See photos of the space on the Tiệm Nướng Trạm Dừng Chill homepage.

The train runs right below us

The charming vintage train on the Da Lat – Trai Mat line runs right below the restaurant. Its whistle echoes through the valley in the middle of your BBQ — a truly different atmosphere.

Trains + sunset

From around 16:30, as the sunset settles over the valley, the trains also start passing the restaurant. Arrive around 16:00 to pick your table and catch both the golden sunset and the vintage train in the same evening.

Trains into the evening

The window for trains passing the restaurant runs until 21:25, so if you stay on into the evening you may still see a train with yellow lights gliding past Da Lat's greenhouse sea of stars — a view very few restaurants have.

180° view tables

Our train-watching tables are set up with a 180° view — so you won't miss a moment of the train.

Train-spotting help

Our staff give you a heads-up when a train is about to arrive — so you have time to grab your phone and film your own viral video.

Fresh, tasty BBQ

Beyond the stunning view, the food is great too. The beef slab with salted egg cheese is our signature dish — everyone orders it.

Tips from the restaurant

Train-spotting tips
for first-timers

Not everyone who comes gets to see a train — these three steps give you the best chance

Arrive before 16:30

The train window starts at 16:30 — come early to get a good view table with nothing blocking it. Between 17:30 and 19:00 we are usually full and don't take new bookings — choose an earlier time or 19:30 onwards.

Book ahead

We get busy, so book ahead via WhatsApp to hold the best train-view spot.

Charge your phone

You'll be shooting lots of videos — charge your phone fully or bring a power bank. We have free power outlets.

Train-spotting safety: this is a working railway with real trains. Watch and film from your table or from behind the restaurant's railing — don't step onto the track or stand close to the rails when a train is coming, and keep small children in their seats.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions
about train-spotting in Da Lat

What time does the train pass the restaurant?

The vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat train passes the Huynh Tan Phat area within the 16:30 – 21:25 window. Sunset also starts around 16:30, so arrive before then to catch both.

Which Da Lat BBQ restaurant has a train view?

Trạm Dừng Chill — 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, Lam Dong. The restaurant sits right on the Da Lat – Trai Mat railway and the vintage train passes right below it, so you can grill BBQ at your table while watching the train and hearing its whistle.

Which tables give the closest view of the train?

The open-air railing row right beside the track is where you see the train closest. The covered railing row higher up looks out over both the sunset and the greenhouse valley, though the train is a little farther away. Tables by the track are limited, so add the note “train view, please” when you book — we hold them in the order bookings come in.

How can I be sure to see the train?

Arrive about 30 minutes before the train window (it starts at 16:30, so be there around 16:00) and book ahead to get a spot with nothing blocking the view. Between 17:30 and 19:00 we are usually full and don't take new bookings.

Can I still go train-spotting when it rains?

Yes. We have a large covered area that still looks out over the valley and the track, so you can watch the trains and enjoy your BBQ as usual in the rain. When it rains we hand out free raincoats; on cold days we provide blankets and charcoal heaters.

How far is the restaurant from the centre, and where can I park?

The restaurant is at 111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, Lam Dong, about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. There is free parking for motorbikes and cars; at weekend peak times you may need to park about 100–200m further away. Grab and taxis can drive right up to the restaurant.

How much does a meal cost per person?

From 95,000 – 300,000 VND per person, VAT included. For a group of 4, that works out to roughly 380,000 – 1,200,000 VND. No surcharge for view tables. We're open 15:00 – 23:00 every day, including weekends and public holidays. We accept cash and bank transfer.
