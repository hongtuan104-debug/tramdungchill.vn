Menu

Da Lat BBQ Restaurant Menu
Trạm Dừng Chill

Fresh every day — from BBQ and steaming hotpots to live seafood. Prices from the menu updated 18/08/2026, VAT included.

The restaurant is open-air, with no glass windows. Tables are set out in rows, many of them running along the railing that overlooks the track of the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat railway and the greenhouse valley. An evening at this scenic Da Lat BBQ restaurant brings three moments: golden afternoon light from when we open at 15:00, then sunset from around 16:30; the vintage Da Lat – Trai Mat train passing by between 16:30 and 21:25; and the greenhouse "sea of stars" lighting up from around 18:30.

Spending is usually 95,000 - 300,000 VND per person (VAT included). The prices printed on each menu page below are per-dish prices, not per-person prices.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions
about the menu

What's on the Trạm Dừng Chill menu?

The menu has over 70 dishes, grouped as follows: BBQ (beef belly, beef slab with salted egg cheese, Korean-style pork belly, rib skewers, chicken feet, chicken wings, frog), grilled seafood (shrimp, squid, octopus, sturgeon, stuffed snails), hotpot (chicken with é leaves, seafood, sturgeon), rice, noodles and sides (fried rice, stir-fried noodles, crispy shrimp rolls, sweet potato balls, salad, kimchi) and drinks (fruit tea, soda, beer, wine, apricot wine, soju).

What is Trạm Dừng Chill's signature dish?

The grilled beef slab with salted egg cheese, which comes in two versions: Kobe beef at 209,000 VND and Okachi beef at 229,000 VND — our best-seller and signature dish, often mentioned by guests and widely shared on TikTok.

How much does a meal cost per person?

Spending is usually 95,000 – 300,000 VND per person, VAT included. We serve à la carte only, with no combos or fixed set menus — you pay only for what you order. Individual dish prices (not counting the 2,000 VND wet towel) range from 12,000 VND (mineral water) to 320,000 VND (a seafood or sturgeon hotpot); sides start at 20,000 VND (extra vegetables); the signature grilled beef slab with salted egg cheese is 209,000 VND (Kobe) or 229,000 VND (Okachi).

Is 12,000 or 20,000 VND the price per person?

No. That is the price of a single item — for example one drink or one side dish. Actual spending is 95,000 – 300,000 VND per person, VAT included, for a full meal of grilled dishes and drinks. For a group of 4, that works out to roughly 380,000 – 1,200,000 VND, depending on how much you order.

How should a large group order?

We don't sell fixed combos — every dish is ordered individually, so you choose exactly the dishes and quantities you want and never pay for food you won't eat. Large groups can just call +84 989 765 070 and we'll advise how much to order.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

We don't have a separate vegetarian menu. Sides with no meat in their names include Extra Vegetables (20,000 VND), Kimchi (30,000 VND), Sweet Potato Balls and French Fries (70,000 VND), and Garden Salad with Vinaigrette (95,000 VND); we have not confirmed that these are fully vegetarian, so vegetarians should ask our staff before ordering. The rest of the menu is meat BBQ, seafood and hotpot.

Is there an English menu?

Yes. The menu is translated into English for international guests, and our staff speak basic English.

Do prices include VAT, and how can I pay?

All menu prices include VAT, and there is no surcharge for view tables. We accept cash and bank transfer.

Where is the restaurant and what are the opening hours?

111 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong - Da Lat Ward, Lam Dong — about 7 km from central Da Lat, roughly a 20-minute drive towards Trai Mat. Open 15:00 – 23:00 every day. Free parking for motorbikes and cars. We have outdoor tables overlooking the valley and a large covered area, so you can still enjoy your BBQ as usual when it rains.
